03 / OWNER PANEL
Your server,
one page.

		
Live numbers and every setting of config.yml in your browser - menus, HUD elements, cutscenes, music zones, server notes and NPC cosmetics with live previews. No account, no port to open: the link is the login.

	

	
    
		
  1. 1
    Get your link
    In game (as op) or in the console run /velobridge panel. You get a link to dev.velo-client.com/panel - it works once, for 15 minutes. In game just click it.
    

    > velobridge panel
Owner panel (15 minutes, once): https://dev.velo-client.com/panel#…
    2. 
		
  2. 2
    Look and change
    Overview shows players online over 24 hours or 30 days, how many use Velo, versions, modules and when people join. The other pages edit everything: settings, mod hints, menus, HUD, cutscenes, music, server notes, NPCs.
    3. 
		
  3. 3
    Save
    Press Save to server. While the panel is open your server checks every 5 seconds, writes the change into config.yml (comments stay) and applies it - the bar turns to Live on your server ✓. A session lasts 12 hours; after that run the command again.
    4. 
	

	

		
What can't the panel change?
Console actions in menus (menus.allow-console-actions) - only the file on your server can switch those on, so a leaked link can never run console commands. Everything else in config.yml is editable.

		
Is the panel safe?
Your server proves who it is with a secret it made itself (plugins/VeloBridge/panel-secret.txt - keep it private). Links are single-use and expire after 15 minutes, sessions after 12 hours. Don't share a fresh link.

		
How do I dress up my NPCs?
Open NPC cosmetics: your FancyNpcs and Citizens NPCs are listed by name (or add any player NPC by UUID). Pick cloaks, wings, auras, bodywear and pets like in Velo's Locker with a 3D preview, or upload your own cloak PNG. Save, and Velo players see them on your server.

		
Can I turn it off?
Yes: panel.enabled: false in config.yml. Then nothing is synced and /velobridge panel says it's off.

Bukkit.getOnlinePlayers().forEach(p ->
    VeloBridge.waypoint(p, "core", coreLocation, "Enemy core", "#e0262e", 0));


// Send the player to the lobby they reach fastest - no proxy.
List<String> lobbies = List.of("eu.lobby.gg", "us.lobby.gg", "asia.lobby.gg");
VeloBridge.pings(player, lobbies).thenAccept(ms -> {
    String best = ms.entrySet().stream()
        .filter(e -> e.getValue() >= 0)
        .min(Map.Entry.comparingByValue())
        .map(Map.Entry::getKey).orElse(lobbies.get(0));
    VeloBridge.transfer(player, best, 25565);
});

05 / PROTOCOL
velo:bridge

		
Minestom, Velocity, your own server: one plugin channel, one UTF-8 JSON object per message, the type in "t". Unknown types and fields are ignored on both sides.

	

	

		

			
			

				
				
				
			

		

		

		

	


// Server → client, raw bytes on velo:bridge (no length prefix)

07 / FAQ
Questions server
owners ask.

	

		
Does it change anything for players without Velo?
No. The plugin only ever talks to players who said hello on the Velo channel. Everyone else gets no packets, no messages, nothing.

		
Can it kick or ban players for their mods?
No, on purpose. Mod hints are shown as a heads-up on the player's PC and nothing is enforced, so a wrong match can never cost anyone their account. The only enforced thing is switching off Velo's own modules you listed.

		
What does the owner panel send to Velo?
While panel.enabled is on: your config, live numbers (players online, Velo versions and modules) and the names of players who joined, so the dashboard can show them. Only someone with a fresh link from /velobridge panel can open it. Set panel.enabled: false and nothing is sent.

		
How do players know it's my server and not Velo?
Toasts and progress bars carry your server's name in your colour, and your mod hints say "From <your server>". Velo's own UI never looks like that. Players can also hide your mod hints for good.

		
What do you collect with stats: true?
Your server name, software, Minecraft version, players online, how many use Velo and which Velo versions. No player names, UUIDs or IPs. Set stats: false and nothing is sent.

		
Does it work on Folia and networks?
Yes. It uses Folia's region and entity schedulers (which Paper and Purpur have too) and falls back to Bukkit's on Spigot. On a network, install it on every backend; transfers work between any servers, with or without a proxy.

		
Which versions?
Servers on Minecraft 1.20.5 and newer (Java 21+). Players on any Velo version with Bridge support.
