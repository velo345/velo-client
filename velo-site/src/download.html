Playing in three steps

  1. Install the launcher and sign in with your Microsoft account. Velo never sees your password.
  2. Pick a Minecraft version, or bring your setup over from Lunar Client, Modrinth or CurseForge.
  3. Press Play. Fabric, Java, Sodium and the rest install themselves.
