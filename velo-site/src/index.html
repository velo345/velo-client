FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Velo Client free?

Yes. The launcher, the client and all {{counts.modules}} modules are free. Velo+ and Store cosmetics are optional and never needed to play.

Which Minecraft versions does Velo Client support?

Minecraft Java Edition {{minecraft.version}} on Fabric. The launcher installs the right build for every profile.

Can I keep my own mods?

Yes. Every profile is a normal Fabric instance: add mods from Modrinth inside the launcher or drop in your own jars. The Velo mod also works in any other Fabric launcher.

Is Velo Client allowed on servers?

The client is made for normal play on any server. Always follow the rules of the server you join.

Can I move over from Lunar Client, Modrinth or CurseForge?

Yes. The launcher finds your existing Lunar, Modrinth and CurseForge setups and imports their mods, settings and servers in a few clicks.

What is Velo+?

An optional monthly subscription: World Hosting for your friends, Cloud Sync with restore points, Clips Pro (1080p60 replay buffer and share links), animated Name Styles, animated custom capes, {{plus.monthlyCoins}} Velo Coins every month, the + badge and the VIP role on Discord. Cancel any time.

How do I get Velo Coins?

Earn them free from daily rewards, quests and inviting friends, get them every month with Velo+, or buy a coin pack.

Does Velo Client run on Mac?

The launcher is available for Windows and Linux. On macOS you can use the Velo mod jars in any Fabric launcher.
