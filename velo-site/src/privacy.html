3. This website

3.1 Server log files

When you visit velo-client.com, our web server records technical data for every request: IP address, date and time, requested address and status, amount of data, referring page and browser/operating system. We need this to deliver the site and to detect and stop attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest.

3.2 No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. All fonts and files are served from our own server - nothing is loaded from Google Fonts, CDNs or social networks.

3.3 Store sign-in

To buy on this website you enter a one-time code from the launcher. We then link this browser to your Minecraft account with a random session token, which your browser keeps in its local storage (not a cookie) for up to 7 days or until you sign out. This storage is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Before a sign-in, your browser solves a small computing puzzle to show it's not a bot; this happens entirely between your browser and our server, with no third party and no tracking.

3.4 Downloads

Download buttons lead to files hosted on GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA). When you download, GitHub processes your IP address and request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

4. The Velo Network (game and launcher)

The Velo Network is on by default and can be switched off in game (Velo Network module) or in the launcher's settings. While it's on, we process the following to provide the features you use (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • Identity: your Minecraft UUID and username, checked through Mojang's session server like a Minecraft server does.
  • Presence: that you are online with Velo, in the game or the launcher, and - if you share it with friends - which server, world or Realm you're on. "Appear offline" hides this. Live sessions are kept in memory only.
  • Friends, messages and parties: friend list, requests, blocks, direct messages (the last 300 per conversation), party membership and positions you share, waypoints you share.
  • Cosmetics: the cosmetics you own and wear, and - if "Share Custom Cape" is on - the cape image you upload, which other Velo players can see. Velo+ members' name style (colours and effect) is shown to other Velo players.
  • Cloud Sync (Velo+): if you turn it on, backups of your Velo settings, HUD layouts, profiles, waypoints and keybinds, with the time, size and the name of the PC each came from. Kept until you delete them, or 90 days after your membership ends. Account files and sign-in data are never included.
  • World Hosting: while you host, your world's name, who you invited or let in, its join code and whether it's listed in Discover; while you play in someone's hosted world, your connection is relayed through our server (Minecraft's own encryption stays end to end, we only pass the data on). Nothing about a hosted world is kept after it closes.
  • Shared clips (Velo+): only clips you choose to share: the video, its thumbnail, title, length, server address if you leave it in, and a view count. Anyone with the link can watch it. You can delete it at any time; otherwise it's deleted 30 days after your membership ends.
  • Velo Coins, Store and rewards: balance, a ledger of every change (purchases, gifts, rewards, refunds, grants), owned items, wishlist, invites, and for daily rewards your play time per day and totals of Minecraft's own statistics (e.g. blocks mined, distance travelled).
  • Reports: when you send a bug report or report a player, its content and the technical details you include.
  • Usage statistics: when your account first and last used Velo, on which days and in which app (game, launcher, website), and how many players are online, sampled every 5 minutes. We use this to plan capacity and improve Velo (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is not shared with anyone.
  • Security: IP addresses and request times for rate limiting and abuse prevention (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), kept in memory and in the 14-day server logs.

6. Rewarded ads (ayeT-Studios)

Only if you choose "Watch an ad" for free coins: an ad page from ayeT-Studios GmbH (Germany) opens in your browser. ayeT-Studios and its advertising partners may process your IP address, device and browser data and your Minecraft UUID (used as an ID so the reward reaches you), and may use cookies to show and measure ads and prevent fraud (their privacy policy). This only happens when you start an ad (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We only receive a confirmation and transaction ID to credit the coins.

7. Discord

If you link your Discord account (to get the Velo Neon cape, the VIP role or staff roles), we store your Discord user ID and name together with your Minecraft UUID, and our Discord bot assigns roles on our server (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Using Discord itself is subject to Discord's privacy policy. You can ask us to unlink you at any time.
