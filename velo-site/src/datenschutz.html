2. Overview

This policy covers two things: this website, and the online features of the Velo Client mod and launcher (the "Velo Network": friends, messages, capes, Velo Coins and daily rewards). Velo Client's offline features (modules, HUD, settings, profiles) run entirely on your computer and send nothing to us.

3. This website

3.1 No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or advertising. Fonts, styles and scripts are served from this site's own hosting. Nothing is loaded from third-party CDNs.

4. Velo Network (in the mod and launcher)

The Velo Network is turned on by default and can be switched off in game (Velo Network module) or in the launcher settings. When it is on, we process the following data to provide the features you use (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • Identity: your Minecraft UUID and username. We check them the same way Minecraft servers do (Mojang's session server). We never receive your Microsoft password or Minecraft access token.
  • Presence: that you are online with Velo Client and, if you share it with friends, which server, world or Realm you are playing on. "Appear offline" hides this. Online status is kept in memory only.
  • Friends and messages: your friend list, requests, blocks, direct messages (the last 300 per conversation) and waypoints you share.
  • Cosmetics: the cape you have equipped and, if "Share Custom Cape" is on, the cape image you uploaded.
  • Velo Coins and rewards: your coin balance, a history of every change (purchases, rewards, refunds, grants), the cosmetics you own, and for daily rewards: how long you played that day plus totals of Minecraft's own statistics (mobs defeated, blocks mined and placed, distance travelled).
  • Technical data: IP address and request times, used for security and rate limiting (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We keep this data while you use the Velo Network. You can ask us to delete your account data at any time (see section 7). Records of purchases are kept for as long as tax and commercial law requires (usually up to 10 years under German law, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Velo Coins purchases (Tebex)

Velo Coins packs are sold through Tebex Limited (United Kingdom), which acts as reseller and merchant of record. When you buy coins, the checkout opens on Tebex's website, and Tebex processes your payment and billing data under its own privacy policy: tebex.io/legal/privacy. We never see your card or bank details. Tebex sends us the order number, transaction ID, amount, currency and status (paid, refunded, disputed) so we can add coins to your account or take them back after a refund (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

6. Optional rewarded ads (ayeT-Studios)

If you choose "Watch an ad" for free coins, an ad page opens in your browser. The video ads are provided by ayeT-Studios GmbH (Germany) and the advertising partners it works with. They may process your IP address, device and browser data and your Minecraft UUID (used as an anonymous ID so the reward reaches your account), and may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See ayeT-Studios' privacy policy. This only happens when you start an ad yourself (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and you can simply not use this feature. We only receive a confirmation that an ad was completed, with a transaction ID, so that we can credit the coins.

7. Other services the launcher talks to

  • Microsoft / Xbox Live and Mojang: sign-in and Minecraft sessions, under Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Modrinth: searching for and downloading mods, under Modrinth's privacy policy.
  • Minecraft servers you add: the launcher pings them directly to show player counts.
  • GitHub: checking for and downloading updates.
