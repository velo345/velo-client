Refunds & Velo+

The short, plain-language version of sections 6–9 of our Terms · Last updated {{legal.lastUpdated}}

Velo Coins

  • Coin packs are digital content delivered right away. At checkout you agree to immediate delivery and lose the 14-day right of withdrawal once the coins are on your account.
  • Something went wrong - you paid but no coins arrived, or you were charged twice? Write to {{legal.email}} with your Minecraft name and the Tebex transaction ID, and we'll sort it out.
  • If a payment is refunded or charged back, the coins from it are removed again.

Store items

  • Items bought with coins can't be exchanged back for coins. Try them on your own skin in 3D first - in the launcher, in game or here on the website.
  • Items stay yours, even if they later leave the Store.

Velo+

  • Monthly, cancel any time. Velo+ renews every 30 days until you cancel. Cancel through the link in your Tebex receipt email, or write to us - it ends at the end of the period you've paid for.
  • What happens when it ends: World Hosting, Cloud Sync, Clips Pro, Name Styles and animated custom capes switch off and the + badge and VIP role are removed. Your cloud backups are kept for 90 days in case you come back. Coins you've received and items you've bought stay yours.
  • Refunds: because Velo+ starts immediately (and the month's coins are credited right away), started periods aren't refunded, unless the law requires it. If something on our side kept you from using Velo+, contact us - we'll make it right.
