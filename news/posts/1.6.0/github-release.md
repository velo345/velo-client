## Velo Client 1.6 - a whole new look

**New glass menus**
- R-Shift is now a glass drawer that is also the HUD editor: drag, scale and click HUD elements, slide it away, or grow it into the full menu with category pills, module cards and live previews
- Every Velo window, the pause menu, the emote wheel, the Store, the Locker and friends share one glass design: the game stays sharp, only the panels are frosted
- Theme settings: accent colour, glass darkness, blur and roundness, live in every menu
- New title screen, Customize and Cloud Sync redesigned

**New launcher**
- New Home with your 3D player, running games, friends and news; glass pages; a new Theme editor
- Instant Play (Settings, off by default): the game waits hidden at its title screen so Play opens it at once (Minecraft 1.21.1 - 26.2)
- Game priority boost for Windows (Settings, on by default): faster loading and steadier frames next to other programs
- The Linux .deb carries the full version number

**Game**
- Tier Tagger: PvPTiers and SubTiers above heads and in the tab list
- Velo Turbo, Velo Rewind and the account switcher on Minecraft 1.21.11
- Faster HUD (HUD Canvas) and two Sodium options: Terrain Culling Window and Leaf Culling Depth
- Armor Bar: Move Up With Extra Hearts, Hide When the Server Hides the Hearts
- Store: cosmetics 30% cheaper, more daily quests, free coins strip with Claim buttons; Velo+ gives 3,000 coins a month; 1,000 Velo Coins welcome gift for everyone

**Fixes**
- Minecraft 1.21.1: the Velo loading screen now finishes
- Doctor knows the inotify limit crash and packs that need a newer Minecraft
- Many visual fixes in the launcher and the menus (Home dock, profile switcher, world map, pause menu blur, second skin layer)
