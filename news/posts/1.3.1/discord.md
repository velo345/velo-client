# 🎬 Velo Client 1.3.1 is out!

**Velo Rewind is now ready for real videos.**

**🎞️ Exact export**
• Renders **frame by frame**: no lost frames, exact timing, voice chat in sync
• Uses your **GPU encoder**: much faster
• **Cancel** or **Stop & keep** any time (also Esc)
• Choose your **system audio device and microphone**

**🙈 Names gone, really**
• Hidden name tags now work on players, incl. the **score line** under names
• Rank / money **holograms** above hidden players disappear too
• New: **Hide on everyone** per clip

**🎥 Smoother replays**
• Camera follow doesn't fly through walls any more
• Click a player in the Players menu to fly to them

**🤖 AI access**
• A much clearer guide, "self" as follow target, all players of a recording, sound settings
• Start a profile from the command line: `--launch <profile>`
• Hidden game no longer captures the mouse on Windows
