# 🎶 Velo Client 1.4.1 is out!

**Lyrics in the sky and a brand-new Video Player.**

**🎤 Lyrics** (new module)
• The lyrics of the song playing on Spotify, synced line by line
• Huge in the sky (behind mountains and clouds), floating in front of you, or on screen
• 19 presets + the **Lyrics Studio** with a live preview: fonts, gradients, rainbow, glow, outline, karaoke fill, 26 animations

**📺 Video Player** (was the YouTube overlay)
• YouTube, **Shorts** (scroll with a key), **Twitch** live streams, any video link and **your own video files**
• New look, controls on hover, picture-in-picture, open with **U**

**🧩 New modules**
• Mini Player, Inventory HUD, Module List, Server Display, Custom Text, Custom Image
• **Motion Blur** (real camera blur) and **TNT Optimizer** - both off by default

**⚔️ 1.8.9**
• Right Shift menu, HUD editor, Locker, Freelook, Hit Color, 1.7 Animations and 17 modules

**🔧 Fixes**
• Color Grading black screen, invisible primed TNT, Windows media controls
• Anti Farm Lag is now off for everyone (opt-in)
• Report a bug can record your lag spikes
• Crash fixes when changing worlds / closing the game
