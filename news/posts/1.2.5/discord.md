# 🚀 Velo Client 1.2.5 is out!

**Packs, repairs and a launcher that just works.** Plus another big FPS jump on Vulkan.

**🎨 Pack Library**
Browse and install **Modrinth resource packs right in the game**: Resource Packs → **Get packs**. Search, sort, one click on Install, done.

**🛠️ Repair Velo**
Something broken? **Settings → Repair** (also in the crash window) puts the current mod back into every profile, clears the cache and can reinstall the launcher. Worlds and settings stay untouched.

**⚡ +17% FPS on Vulkan**
Minecraft drew two invisible full-screen layers every single frame. Velo now skips them: **2690 → 3160 FPS** in our 26.2 benchmark, smoother lows too.

**📦 Switching to Velo**
• **Import from OneClient**: mods, worlds, settings, servers
• Imports now bring your **worlds** by default
• CurseForge on other drives is found

**🔄 Better updates**
• Broken downloads continue and retry by themselves
• Windows: **no more clicking through the installer**, Velo restarts on its own
• Live progress with speed & time left, plus a log if anything goes wrong

**🔧 Fixed**
• Black/white screen on 1.21.x after the 1.2.2 update
• Flickering hotbar with VulkanMod
• Launcher buttons hidden under the taskbar on small screens
• Removing a mod silently failing
• Crash with old Lunar library files
• Lots more from your bug reports 💙
