# 🚀 Velo Client 1.2.6 is out!

**Item farms without the lag.** Thousands of dropped items no longer kill your FPS.

**📦 Item farm mode (Anti Farm Lag)**
When lots of items are lying around or drifting in water streams, Velo **groups them** and shows a **count over each group** (x37). Items close to you stay fully detailed.
• **97 → 408 FPS** in our test with ~3000 items, 1% lows 42 → 110
• **Max Drawn Items**: 1000 by default, adjustable
• Labels your way: **font, background, size**, or turn them off

**⚡ Faster HUD**
• The whole HUD is reused when nothing changed
• Velo checks by itself whether Gnetum's HUD caching helps on your PC

**🔧 Fixed**
• **Main menu:** if the Velo title screen fails (GPU drivers, other mods), Minecraft's menu shows instead of a black screen
• Game hanging on Windows with 1.21.x (Ixeris)
• "Could not reserve enough space": the Doctor now explains it and sets fitting RAM

💡 Anti Farm Lag is off by default: turn it on in the **Performance** tab.
