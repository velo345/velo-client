# ✨ Velo Client 1.4 is out!

**A whole new look: a new launcher, new in-game menus, and a world to explore on your Home page.**

**🖥️ New launcher design**
• New Home with an animated night-time horizon, your skin standing on the ridge
• Sidebar, new fonts, calmer colours on every page
• Updates now show a proper "what's new" window with **Restart to update**

**🎮 Velo World** (press WASD on Home)
• A side-scroller right in the launcher: biomes, caves, dungeons and bosses
• Flowing water and lava, swimming, a trader once you have an iron pickaxe
• Play it together with your party
• Snake and Blockfall, with new chill music

**🧩 New in-game menus** (Right Shift)
• Modules open with one click, three card sizes, cleaner Edit HUD button
• **Live previews for every module that changes how something looks**
• Locker and Store like in the launcher: one click to wear, right click for more
• Friends in one window with chat side by side

**🎬 Velo Rewind**
• You're back in your own recordings (and in the players list)
• Recording keeps going when a server moves you (lobby → arena)

**🔧 More**
• Better Tab List slides open and closed
• Launcher Doctor fixes more crashes on its own (Sodium/Iris, narrator, blocked sound library, broken shader packs)
• Faster HUD and Store, friends' chat, version picker and many small fixes
