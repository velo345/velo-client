# 🎬 Velo Client 1.3 is out: Velo Rewind!

**Record your Minecraft moments in 3D, edit them and export them as a video. All inside Velo.**

**⏺️ Velo Rewind**
• **Record** with F6 or from the pause menu. Follows you across server switches
• Replays are **3D**: fly anywhere (through blocks too) and watch from any angle
• **Timeline** with clips and **keyframes**: camera path, angle, roll, FOV, time of day, rain, fog, speed
• **Camera follow**: keeps a player or item in view
• **Players menu**: search, hide anyone, click to fly to them
• Per player: hide **cloak, cosmetics, pets, name tag, glow** or swap the **skin**

**🎥 Export with sound**
• **YouTube 16:9**, **TikTok 9:16**, square, up to 1440p / 60 FPS
• Records **Simple Voice Chat** (each player on their own track), optional mic and Discord audio
• **Volume per source** with keyframes
• Clean video: no HUD, no chat, no menus
• **Green screen**, blue screen or black background for editing

**💾 Never lost**
• Recordings come back even after a crash
• Edits save themselves, with **Undo / Redo** and backups

**👤 Account switcher**
Switch accounts **right on the title screen**, no restart.

**🧩 New modules**
• **Numbered Inventories**: numbers on every slot (from 0, like plugin configs)
• **Color Codes** (F4): pick colours, copy as hex / &-code / MiniMessage, gradients
• **Shield Indicator**
• **Own Name Tag** now on for everyone

**🤖 AI access** (at your own risk)
Launcher → Settings → **AI Access**: let AI agents edit replays, export videos, record and more through a local API. Playing only in singleplayer or as an operator.

**✨ Also new**
• Emoji suggestions as soon as you type **:**
• Map opens centred on you
• Your cosmetics show for players on other clients
• New **LuckySMP cloak**
• Launcher: instant Play feedback, no double starts, comes back when the game closes, download mirror

**🔧 Fixed**
• Connection errors on some servers (IPv6)
• Performance Boost settings not kept
• Chat refresh hang, title screen text on some Windows PCs
• Lots more from your bug reports 💙
